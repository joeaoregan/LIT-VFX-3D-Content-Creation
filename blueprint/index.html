01 · Built on a Chevy Impala Chassis

Production designer Anton Furst built the 1989 Batmobile on a stretched 1967 Chevrolet Impala chassis powered by a Chevy V8, stretched to over 6.6 metres long to give it that unmistakable long-bonnet silhouette.

02 · Real Harrier & Jet Parts

To make the turbine intake look authentic, Furst's crew used real aircraft components—including a British Harrier jump-jet nose cone and a genuine jet engine turbine—for the front intake and rear exhaust.

03 · Pinewood Studios Gotham

Tim Burton's 1989 Batman was filmed almost entirely on massive backlot sets at Pinewood Studios in the UK, where Anton Furst won the Academy Award for Best Art Direction.
